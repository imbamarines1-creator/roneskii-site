Privacy Policy

This policy describes the current public website for Finds By Roneskii and the intended private use of Roneskii Pin Studio. Please review and update it if new features or third-party services are added.

Information collected

The public website does not currently provide account registration, contact forms, or newsletter subscriptions. The site itself does not intentionally collect names, email addresses, or payment details from visitors. The hosting provider may process standard technical request data, such as IP addresses and browser information, to deliver and secure the website.

Cookies and analytics

The current website does not intentionally install its own tracking cookies or analytics scripts. External services, hosting providers, or linked retailers may use their own cookies or technical processing under their policies.

Affiliate links

Some pages may link to Amazon or other retailers. Following those links takes you to third-party websites that have their own privacy policies. As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.

Pinterest integration

Roneskii Pin Studio is intended for private use by the operator of the Finds By Roneskii Pinterest Business account. If Pinterest API access is enabled, the application may request authorization to read the operator's own boards and Pins and create Pins on the operator's behalf. Any access tokens should be stored securely, not embedded in public website code. The current public website does not connect visitors' Pinterest accounts or request their Pinterest data.

Data retention and security

The current public website has no visitor account database. Private local editing files remain under the operator's control. If a server-side Pinterest integration is added, this policy will be revised to describe token storage, retention, deletion, and applicable service providers.

Your choices

You can choose not to follow external affiliate links. For information held by third-party sites, consult their respective privacy notices.

Policy updates

We may update this policy when site functionality changes. The updated date will appear at the top of this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the operator through the official Finds By Roneskii Pinterest profile. A dedicated contact email can be added when available.
